# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be decent to people. Assume good faith, critique the work rather than the person, and
accept that a maintainer may say no to your idea without it being a judgement about you.

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening an issue, or privately via a
[security advisory](https://github.com/Dibakar01/graph-sorcerer/security/advisories/new) if it is
sensitive. Reports are handled confidentially.
